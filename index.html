Our Story

Morphis Veterinary Services, Inc. is a Christian, family-owned practice serving Seminole, Oklahoma since 2018. We treat every patient, and every pet owner, like family.

Meet the Vets

Dr. Laura Morphis

Dr. Laura Morphis is a 1996 graduate of Oklahoma State University's veterinary program, bringing over 30 years of experience to every patient she sees.

Dr. Baylee Morphis

Dr. Baylee Morphis, Dr. Laura's daughter, is a 2026 graduate of Ross University School of Veterinary Medicine, carrying the family's tradition of care into the next generation.
